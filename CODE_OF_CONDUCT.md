# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone who takes part in the BabelTower project — in the source repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space. It governs human collaboration on the project. Conduct on the hosted Service is governed separately by the [Acceptable Use Policy](ACCEPTABLE_USE.md).

## Our Standard

Be direct, kind, and specific. We want BabelTower to be boring, inspectable, and useful, and we want contributing to it to feel the same way.

Expected behavior:

- Assume good faith and engage with the strongest version of an argument.
- Give and accept feedback gracefully; critique the work, not the person.
- Respect that maintainers have limited time and that "no" is a complete answer.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, hate speech, threats, or intimidation.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or advances; sexual abuse of any kind.
- Doxxing or publishing others' private information without consent.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, issues, or reviews.
- Encouraging or excusing any of the above.

## Scope

This Code applies in all project spaces and when an individual is representing the project in public. It also applies to private interactions between participants when they relate to the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may warn, edit or remove contributions, lock or close discussions, or block participants who violate this Code, with or without prior notice. Decisions aim to keep the project safe and productive.

## Reporting

Report concerns privately to the maintainer at security@babel-tower.com. Reports will be handled discreetly and in good faith. Do not use public issues to report a conduct concern about a specific person.

## Attribution

This Code is intentionally short and is inspired by the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
